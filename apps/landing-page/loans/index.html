      Step 4 of 5

      Terms & Acknowledgement of Debt

      A plain-language summary of what you're agreeing to.

      Telling us if you change jobs

      If you leave or change your employer, let Diamond Bank know in writing straight away so repayment arrangements can be updated.

      Repayment by salary deduction

      You authorise your employer to deduct the agreed instalment from your salary each month and pay it over to Diamond Bank until the loan is settled.

      If payments are missed

      If an instalment is missed, the full outstanding balance may become due immediately and interest continues to run on what is owed.

      Fees and interest

      Interest and the establishment, application, insurance and transfer fees shown in your quote form part of the amount you repay.

      Collection and legal costs

      If recovery action becomes necessary, you agree to cover reasonable collection charges and legal costs on the attorney-and-client scale.

      No waiver

      If Diamond Bank allows extra time or relaxes a term once, that does not give up any of its rights under this agreement.

      Where disputes are heard

      You consent to any dispute being heard in the Magistrates' Court, without preventing Diamond Bank from approaching the High Court.

      Checking and sharing your information

      You allow Diamond Bank to verify the information you have given and to share it with banks and the Credit Bureau where necessary.

      The whole agreement

      This Acknowledgement of Debt, together with the loan agreement, is the complete agreement between you and Diamond Bank.
